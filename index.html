When the week changes

  • Move it. Open a session and use "Move to" to put it on another day, or drop it this week. Finished sessions stay put.
  • Shift, don't stack. Missed something? Move it to the next free day, or let it go. Never double up to catch up.
  • No hard days back to back. Give your body a day between the toughest sessions.
  • Always warm up. Three minutes or so. Short enough that there's no excuse to skip it.
  • Bigger changes? Tell Tobias and the plan gets updated.
