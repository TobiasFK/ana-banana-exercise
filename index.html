    When the week changes

    • Just tell the box at the top. Type what changed and the rest of the week is rebuilt around it. Finished sessions stay put.
    • Shift, don't stack. Missed something? It moves to the next free day, or drops. Never double up to catch up.
    • No hard days back to back. Give your body a day between the toughest sessions.
    • Always warm up. Three minutes or so. Short enough that there's no excuse to skip it.
    • Bigger changes? Use "Adjust the whole plan" further down.
